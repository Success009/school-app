Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Udayashree English School mobile application and official web portal (https://school.success0.com.np) operated for students, parents, faculty, and administrative staff.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential academic and contact information necessary to provide educational communication and academic records:

  • Phone Number: Used strictly for secure SMS OTP authentication and attendance/grade alerts.
  • Student & Academic Records: Attendance, marks, class timetables, fee receipts, and school notices.
  • Device Tokens: Used exclusively to deliver school announcements and emergency notices via push notifications.

2. Use of Information

The information collected is used solely to:

  • Authenticate verified students, parents, and teachers into their designated portals.
  • Deliver real-time school announcements, attendance status, and exam results.
  • Enable offline access to student report cards and academic timetables.

3. Data Protection and Security

All data transmitted between the application and school servers is encrypted using modern Industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS). We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to any third-party advertisers or commercial entities.

4. Device Permissions

Our application requests minimal device permissions strictly on demand:

  • Notifications: To alert parents and students of urgent school announcements.
  • Internet Access: To synchronize records with the school database.
  • File Access: Triggered strictly via the standard system file picker when uploading or downloading educational documents and receipts. We do not access external storage or sensitive media without user selection.
